Lead Generation: Black Filmmakers for Festival Outreach

The brief

Find 20 Black filmmakers in the USA, Canada or the UK for film festival outreach. Each needed fewer than 10,000 Instagram followers, at least one finished film, and a location I could confirm. For every lead: name, handle, profile link, follower count, country, most recent film, a trailer or promo link, and a short description of their work.

How I found and checked the leads

  • Built a shortlist from Black film festival line-ups, filmmaker programmes and film press
  • Opened every Instagram profile to record the live follower count and bio
  • Matched each person's most recent film to a trailer or promo post
  • Confirmed location and identity from their own bio, festival pages or press
  • Set aside about 10 profiles: over 10,000 followers, inactive, private or the wrong person
  • Flagged weaker leads and credits to double-check, instead of padding the list

Inbox Zero: Cleaning Up a 10,000-Email Gmail Inbox

Email management · Gmail labels, filters and Google Apps Script · November 2026

Email management
10,095 → 0Emails sitting in the inbox
8Labels, one for each type of email
0Emails deleted: all still searchable
1Script to sort the backlog in one run

The problem

Over 10,000 emails had built up in the inbox. Job alerts, bank notices, training updates and subscriptions were all mixed together, so important messages were easy to miss and hard to find again.

What I did

  • Grouped senders by type and created labels: job platforms, banking, training, subscriptions and an "Others" catch-all
  • Wrote a Google Apps Script that labelled every old email and moved it out of the inbox in batches
  • Set up Gmail filters so new emails from known senders skip the inbox and land in their label
  • Archived instead of deleting, so every email stays searchable in All Mail

Before and after

What this means for a client
  • Nothing important gets buried: the inbox only holds what still needs action.
  • Anything can be found in seconds, by label or by search.
  • It stays clean on its own: filters sort new mail as it arrives.
